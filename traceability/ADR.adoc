= Traceability

Two implementations:

* INVOLVED
* INVOLVED_WITH_FULL_DETAILS

== Implementations

=== INVOLVED

- Only store aggregateId with whom (ExecutedBy);
- No duplicates;

**Pros**

- limited logging.

**Cons**

- do not know when.

=== INVOLVED_WITH_FULL_DETAILS

- Each time an aggregateId is mutated, accessed a log will be added.
- Database will always increase.

== UseCases

=== Appending

- Appending is added to the CommandHandler side.

=== Finding

- Finding by AnyAggregateId or ExecutedByHashed;
- When finding by AnyAggregateId, Compound aggregateIds are returned to if queried to via **includeUncompounded**;
- Finding by AnyAggregateId should take a list too, and in this case (same for other, return the aggregateId per line).
